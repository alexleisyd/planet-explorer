Planet Explorer

Privacy Policy

Planet Explorer collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to do either. This page explains why that is a statement about how the app is built rather than a promise about how it is run.

Summary. No accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No third-party services. No network requests of any kind. The app requests no Android permissions. Everything you do stays on your device.

What the app does not collect

Planet Explorer does not collect, transmit, store off-device, sell, or share any personal or device information. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any account or contact information — there are no accounts.
  • Location of any kind, precise or approximate.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or diagnostics.
  • Usage or analytics data. Nothing about what you look at is recorded anywhere but your own device.
  • Photos, files, contacts, microphone or camera input. The app never asks for access to any of these.

Why it cannot collect anything

The app is a self-contained offline program. It ships as one HTML file and one graphics library, both packaged inside the app, and every world you fly to is generated on your device as you arrive rather than downloaded. The app makes no network requests, contains no third-party SDKs, and declares no Android permissions at all — not even INTERNET. You can verify that last point yourself: the permission list on the app's Google Play listing is empty, and the source is public.

What is stored on your device

So that the game remembers you between sessions, three small values are saved in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • the weight and age you optionally type into the pilot row, used to work out what you would weigh on other worlds;
  • which worlds you have surveyed, so your mission badges persist;
  • whether you left the body-data panel open or folded.

These never leave your device and are not readable by other apps. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android's app settings, deletes them permanently. There is nothing to request from us, because we never have it.

Children

The app is suitable for children and is designed to be safe for them by construction rather than by policy: there is no advertising, no in-app purchase, no chat or user-generated content, no external links, and no data collection of any kind. It complies with the Google Play Families policy and, having no data collection, raises no obligations under COPPA or the GDPR's provisions for children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collected or transmitted anything, this page would be updated before that version was published, and the change would be described in the app's release notes. The date below is when this page was last revised.
